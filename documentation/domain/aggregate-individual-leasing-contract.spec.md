# Domain Specification – IndividualLeasingContract

## Purpose

`IndividualLeasingContract` is the **Einzel-Leasingvertrag (ELV)**: one employee's
lease of one bike, issued under a master leasing contract (LRV).

It enforces the lease lifecycle, the arithmetic relating the bike's value to the
monthly rate and the residual value, and the commercial constraints inherited from
the master contract at issue time.


## 1. Aggregate Root

Name:
`IndividualLeasingContract`

Description:
Represents one lease, identified by an `IndividualLeasingContractId` and addressed
in the business by its `ElvNumber`. Carries exactly one current `IlcConfiguration`.

Consistency boundary:

**Inside**: the lease and its `IlcConfiguration`, for the same reason the master
contract holds its own — the terms and the contract they belong to change together
or not at all (`modelling.definition.md`, Aggregate).

**Referenced by id, outside the boundary**:
- `masterLeasingContractId` — a plain `String`. **ADR 0005 category 2**: owned by
  the `masterleasing` context, opaque here. Never parsed, never branched on, never
  used to reconstruct `MasterLeasingContractId`.
- `employerId`, `lessorId` — `shared.domain` value objects (category 3).
- `jobCyclistId`, `bikeId`, `kauNumber` — external identities referenced by this
  context only, so Value Objects declared here (**category 4**).

**Not modelled**: `UEV_CONTRACT` (*Nutzungsüberlassungsvertrag*), the
employer-employee side of the same lease. It has a 1:1 relationship with this
aggregate and no lifecycle of its own, so when it is built it belongs **inside**
this boundary, not beside it (`architecture.definition.md` § 11, Growing the
registry). It is deliberately out of scope today
(`project.definition.md`, Scope).


## 2. Invariants (Always-Valid)

- **I-01**: `elvNumber` matches `ELV-\d{8}` → `ElvNumber` throws
  `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractException`
- **I-02**: `termEnd == termStart plus term.months` → `IlcConfiguration` throws
  `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractException`
- **I-03**: `ratePerMonth == leasingValue × leasingFactor`, rounded HALF_UP to
  scale 4 → `IlcConfiguration` throws
  `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractException`
- **I-04**: `leasingValue` lies within the inherited price band →
  `issue` throws `PriceOutsideContractBandException`
- **I-05**: the master contract is `ACTIVE` at issue time → `issue` throws
  `MasterContractNotActiveException`
- **I-06**: `termStart` is not before the master contract's activation date →
  `issue` throws `TermStartBeforeMasterActivationException`
- **I-07**: activation is permitted only from `PENDING_ACTIVATION`, or is an
  idempotent no-op when already `ACTIVE` → `activate` throws
  `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractStateException`
- **I-08**: termination is permitted only from a state the *terminating party* may
  terminate from — see the guard table in § 3 → `terminate` throws
  `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractStateException`
- **I-09**: `term` is between 12 and 60 months inclusive → `LeaseTerm` throws
  `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractException`
- **I-10**: `leasingValue`, `serviceRate`, `insuranceRate` and `residualValue` are
  non-negative and share one currency → `IlcConfiguration` throws
  `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractException`
- **I-11**: `residualValue <= leasingValue` → `IlcConfiguration` throws
  `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractException`
- **I-12**: `cancellationReason`, when given, is non-blank and at most 400
  characters → `CancellationReason` throws
  `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractException`

**I-02, I-03 and I-11 are the reason this aggregate exists rather than a CRUD
row.** They are arithmetic relationships between fields the data model stores
independently, which means the database can hold a lease whose stated monthly rate
does not follow from its stated value and factor. Nothing outside the aggregate
would notice, and the error is in money.

I-03 fixes rounding at HALF_UP scale 4 rather than leaving it to the caller, because
a rate rounded differently at two call sites produces two contracts that disagree by
cents and no test that compares them
(`modelling.definition.md`, Money).

**I-04 to I-06 are policies, not invariants of this aggregate.** The values they
check against belong to the master contract in another context. They are passed in
as `InheritedLeasingTerms` and the aggregate enforces them against what it was
given; it never reaches for them (`domain-vs-use-case.definition.md` § 4).


## 2a. Value Objects

### IndividualLeasingContractId
- Wraps `UUID`. **Category 1** — owned by this context.
- Generated by the application layer before aggregate creation.

### ElvNumber
- Wraps `String` — the *Einzel-Leasingvertrag* number the business quotes.
- Invariant: matches `ELV-\d{8}` (I-01).
- **Category 1**: this context owns the numbering.
- The format is an invariant rather than a comment because it is what humans use to
  refer to a lease; a malformed one is unsearchable in every downstream system.

### JobCyclistId / BikeId / KauNumber
- Each wraps `String`; each non-blank.
- **Category 4** — external identities (*JobRadler:in*, *Leasingobjekt*,
  *Antragsnummer*) referenced by this context only, so Value Objects here rather
  than `String`s. See ADR 0005 for why category 4 exists.
- Opaque: this context validates non-blankness and nothing about their structure.

### CancellationReason
- Wraps `String` — the *Kündigungsgrund* for **this lease**: employee left, bike
  written off, return quota claimed.
- Invariants: non-blank, at most `MAX_LENGTH = 400` characters (I-12).
- **A different type from `masterleasing`'s of the same name**, deliberately
  (ADR 0003). That one is a lessor's ground for terminating a framework agreement.
- An *absent* reason is the absence of this object — terminating without a reason is
  permitted, terminating with an empty one is not.

### LeaseTerm
- Wraps `Int` months — *Laufzeit*. Invariant: `12..60` (I-09).
- 12 is the shortest term the leasing model supports; 60 the longest any product
  offers. Outside that range the residual-value calculation is not defined.

### LeasingFactor
- Wraps `Percentage` — the share of the leasing value charged per month.
- Invariant: greater than 0 and at most 100.

### ConfigurationVersion
- Wraps `Int`. Invariant: `>= 1`.
- Declared in this context; `masterleasing` has its own. Neither context owns the
  concept of "a version number" and the type has no cross-context contract, so
  sharing it would grow the kernel for nothing (`architecture.definition.md` § 9).

### InheritedLeasingTerms
- Not persisted. A parameter object carrying the values UC05 fetched from
  `masterleasing`'s inport: `priceRange` (as two `Money` values), `masterStatus`
  (whether the master contract is active) and `masterActivationDate`.
- **This is the anti-corruption boundary.** It is declared in *this* context and
  built by *this* context's driver from `LeasingTermsResult`. The aggregate
  therefore depends on a local type, not on `masterleasing`'s result type, and
  enforces I-04 to I-06 against values rather than against another model.

### Enums
- `IndividualLeasingContractStatus`: `PENDING_ACTIVATION`, `ACTIVE`, `TERMINATED`,
  `EXPIRED`
- `ServicePackage`: `NONE`, `BASIC`, `COMFORT`, `PREMIUM` — *Servicepaket*
- `InheritanceMode`: `LIVE_LINKED`, `COPIED_ONCE`. Only `COPIED_ONCE` is
  implemented (`documentation/notes.md`)
- `TerminatedBy`: `LESSEE` (the employee ended this lease, UC07) or
  `MASTER_CONTRACT` (the LRV was cancelled and this lease fell with it, UC08).
  Selects which termination event `terminate(...)` emits — attribution lives in the
  event **type**, not in a payload field, so consumers subscribe to the fact they
  care about instead of filtering

### IlcConfiguration (domain entity, inside the aggregate)

| Property | Type | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| `version` | `ConfigurationVersion` | |
| `inheritanceMode` | `InheritanceMode` | `COPIED_ONCE` in practice |
| `servicePackage` | `ServicePackage` | |
| `leasingValue` | `Money` | the basis for every derived amount |
| `leasingFactor` | `LeasingFactor` | |
| `ratePerMonth` | `Money` | derived; I-03 |
| `serviceRate` | `Money` | *Servicerate* |
| `insuranceRate` | `Money` | *Versicherungsrate* |
| `residualValue` | `Money` | *Restwert*; I-11 |
| `term` | `LeaseTerm` | *Laufzeit* |
| `termStart` | `LocalDate` | |
| `termEnd` | `LocalDate` | derived; I-02 |

`ratePerMonth` and `termEnd` are **stored although derived**, and that needs a
reason rather than a shrug: they are the figures on the contract the parties
signed. Recomputing them on read would mean a change to the rounding rule silently
rewrites history. Storing them and asserting the relationship (I-02, I-03) keeps
the signed values authoritative while making an inconsistent pair unconstructible.


## 3. State Model

Possible states:
- `PENDING_ACTIVATION`
- `ACTIVE`
- `TERMINATED`
- `EXPIRED`

Allowed transitions:
- `PENDING_ACTIVATION → ACTIVE` — `activate` (UC06)
- `PENDING_ACTIVATION → TERMINATED` — `terminate`, either party
- `ACTIVE → TERMINATED` — `terminate`, either party

Illegal transitions:
- `TERMINATED → any` — but a second `MASTER_CONTRACT` termination is an idempotent
  no-op, not a transition and not an error (§ 4); a second `LESSEE` termination throws
- `EXPIRED → any`
- `ACTIVE → PENDING_ACTIVATION`

`EXPIRED` is the lease reaching `termEnd` and is reachable from no function today,
for the same reason `ENDED` is on the master contract: it needs a time-based
trigger. Recorded as open in § 7.

**A lease may be issued before its master contract is active** — that is the whole
point of `PENDING_ACTIVATION`, and it is why UC06 exists. Employers collect
applications while the LRV is still being signed; those leases wait, and activating
the master contract activates all of them.


## 4. Behavior

Public functions:

- `issue(...)` — static factory
- `reconstitute(...)` — static factory, persistence only
- `activate(activatedAt: Instant)`
- `terminate(terminatedAt: Instant, terminatedBy: TerminatedBy, reason: CancellationReason?)`
- `pullDomainEvents(): List<DomainEvent>`

Plus read-only accessors: `contractId`, `elvNumber`, `masterLeasingContractId`,
`employerId`, `lessorId`, `jobCyclistId`, `bikeId`, `kauNumber`, `configuration`,
`status`, `terminatedAt`, `terminatedBy`, `cancellationReason`.

**Mutable (`var`, `private set`) properties**: `status`, `terminatedAt`,
`terminatedBy`, `cancellationReason` — four. Every one must be written by
`IndividualLeasingContractRepository.update`
(`ports/individual-leasing-contract-repository.outport.spec.md`).

### issue(contractId, elvNumber, masterLeasingContractId, employerId, lessorId, jobCyclistId, bikeId, kauNumber, configuration, inheritedTerms, now)

Creates a lease in `PENDING_ACTIVATION`.

Preconditions:
- `inheritedTerms.masterContractActive` → else `MasterContractNotActiveException`
  (I-05)
- `configuration.leasingValue` within `inheritedTerms.priceRange` → else
  `PriceOutsideContractBandException` (I-04)
- `configuration.termStart >= inheritedTerms.masterActivationDate` → else
  `TermStartBeforeMasterActivationException` (I-06)
- the configuration's own invariants (I-02, I-03, I-09 to I-11) hold by construction

Postconditions:
- `status = PENDING_ACTIVATION`
- `terminatedAt`, `terminatedBy`, `cancellationReason` all null

Emitted events:
- `IndividualLeasingContractIssued(contractId, elvNumber, masterLeasingContractId, occurredAt)`

Ordering note: the three inherited-term checks run **before** anything is
constructed, so a rejected lease has no partially built state and no side effect.

### reconstitute(...)

Rebuilds a persisted lease. Preconditions: none by design
(`modelling.definition.md` § Rehydration Rule). Emits nothing.
**Persistence mapper only**, enforced by
`ClassRoleRulesTest.reconstitute_isCalledOnlyByPersistenceMappers`.

### activate(activatedAt)

Transitions `PENDING_ACTIVATION → ACTIVE` when the master contract is activated
(UC06). Triggered by the `MasterLeasingContractActivated` integration event, never
by a mutation.

Preconditions:
- Already `ACTIVE` → **idempotent no-op**: returns early, no event, no state change
- Otherwise `status == PENDING_ACTIVATION` → else
  `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractStateException` (I-07)

Postconditions:
- `status = ACTIVE`

Emitted events:
- `IndividualLeasingContractActivated(contractId, activatedAt)`

Notes:
- **Idempotent because the trigger may be re-delivered.** An `AFTER_COMMIT`
  listener can see the same event twice, and the fan-out runs over every pending
  lease in one transaction — throwing on one already-active lease would roll back
  the whole batch (`architecture.definition.md` § 10). This is the opposite
  decision from `MasterLeasingContract.activate`, which throws on a repeat, and the
  discriminator is the trigger: an operator mutation versus a re-deliverable event.
- `activatedAt` is **not stored**. It is another context's fact, relayed; no
  invariant here compares against it (`modelling.definition.md`, What an aggregate
  stores).

### terminate(terminatedAt, terminatedBy, reason)

Transitions to `TERMINATED`, recording who terminated and optionally why
(UC07, UC08).

**The guard is caller-dependent**, decided by a private `terminableBy` helper so the
permissions read as one table:

| From state | `LESSEE` (UC07) | `MASTER_CONTRACT` (UC08) |
|-----------|-----------------|--------------------------|
| `PENDING_ACTIVATION` | ✔ | ✔ |
| `ACTIVE` | ✔ | ✔ |
| `TERMINATED` | ✖ `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractStateException` | idempotent **no-op** |
| `EXPIRED` | ✖ `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractStateException` | ✖ — a lease that ran its full term cannot be retroactively terminated |

Preconditions:
- `terminatedAt` and `terminatedBy` non-null
- `reason` may be null — terminating without a reason is permitted
- the current state permits *this party* to terminate (table above) → else
  `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractStateException` (I-08)

Postconditions:
- `status = TERMINATED`
- `terminatedAt`, `terminatedBy` and `cancellationReason` **stored on the
  aggregate** — attribution is state this lease owns, and a subsequent rejected
  termination must be able to prove it did not overwrite an existing one

Emitted events, selected by `terminatedBy`:
- `IndividualLeasingContractTerminatedByLessee(contractId, terminatedAt, reason)`
- `IndividualLeasingContractTerminatedByMasterContract(contractId, terminatedAt, reason, masterLeasingContractId)`

Notes:
- **Idempotency is caller-dependent, and the asymmetry is the point.** A second
  `LESSEE` termination throws: it is a deliberate act on an already-terminated
  lease, and the client should be told (`CONFLICT`). A second `MASTER_CONTRACT`
  termination is a no-op, because UC04 fans out over every lease under a contract
  inside one transaction — throwing on a lease somebody had already terminated
  would roll back the whole master-contract cancellation and block the rest.
- Either way the no-op returns **before** mutating, so an existing attribution —
  including a `LESSEE` one — is never overwritten.
- `masterLeasingContractId` appears on the `MASTER_CONTRACT` event but is not
  re-stored by `terminate`; it is already aggregate state from `issue`.

### pullDomainEvents()

Returns an immutable snapshot of recorded events and clears the internal list.


## 5. Domain Events

All implement `shared.domain.event.DomainEvent`. All are immutable `data class`es.
Published post-commit via `DomainEventPublisher` (ADR 0002).

| Event | Trigger | Payload |
|-------|---------|---------|
| `IndividualLeasingContractIssued` | `issue` | `contractId`, `elvNumber`, `masterLeasingContractId`, `occurredAt` |
| `IndividualLeasingContractActivated` | `activate` | `contractId`, `activatedAt` |
| `IndividualLeasingContractTerminatedByLessee` | `terminate(..., LESSEE, ...)` | `contractId`, `terminatedAt`, `reason` (nullable) |
| `IndividualLeasingContractTerminatedByMasterContract` | `terminate(..., MASTER_CONTRACT, ...)` | `contractId`, `terminatedAt`, `reason` (nullable), `masterLeasingContractId` (`String`) |

All four live in
`individualleasing.core.domain.individualleasingcontract.event`. **None is
promoted to `shared.domain.event`**, because no other context consumes them today.
`masterleasing` learns nothing from a lease being issued or terminated — UC04 calls
`individualleasing`'s inport and reads the result synchronously, so it does not need
an event to find out (`modelling.definition.md`, Domain Event).

Two termination events rather than one with a `terminatedBy` field: two events for
two facts. A consumer that cares only about employer-side cancellations subscribes
rather than filters.


## 6. Failure Scenarios

| Scenario | Exception | Thrown by |
|----------|-----------|-----------|
| ELV number malformed | `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractException` | `ElvNumber` |
| `termEnd` inconsistent with `termStart` + term | `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractException` | `IlcConfiguration` |
| `ratePerMonth` inconsistent with value × factor | `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractException` | `IlcConfiguration` |
| Residual value exceeds leasing value | `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractException` | `IlcConfiguration` |
| Mixed currencies within one configuration | `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractException` | `IlcConfiguration` |
| Term outside 12..60 months | `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractException` | `LeaseTerm` |
| Bike price outside the contract's band | `PriceOutsideContractBandException` | `issue` |
| Master contract not active | `MasterContractNotActiveException` | `issue` |
| `termStart` before master activation | `TermStartBeforeMasterActivationException` | `issue` |
| Activate when neither `PENDING_ACTIVATION` nor already `ACTIVE` | `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractStateException` | `activate` |
| `LESSEE` terminate when `TERMINATED` or `EXPIRED` | `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractStateException` | `terminate` |
| `MASTER_CONTRACT` terminate when `EXPIRED` | `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractStateException` | `terminate` — `TERMINATED` is a no-op for this party, not a failure |
| Blank or over-long termination reason | `InvalidIndividualLeasingContractException` | `CancellationReason` |
| Lease not found | `IndividualLeasingContractNotFoundException` | the driver, not the aggregate |


## 7. Test Requirements

### Covered
- [ ] `ElvNumber` format invariant (I-01) — `ElvNumberTest`
- [ ] Configuration arithmetic invariants I-02, I-03, I-10, I-11 —
      `IlcConfigurationTest`, each asserting an **exact** expected value
- [ ] `LeaseTerm` and `LeasingFactor` bounds (I-09) — `LeaseTermTest`,
      `LeasingFactorTest`
- [ ] Inherited-term policies I-04 to I-06 —
      `IndividualLeasingContractTest.issue_*`, including the boundary cases at both
      ends of the price band and `termStart` exactly equal to the activation date
- [ ] `PENDING_ACTIVATION → ACTIVE`, its guard and its idempotency (I-07) —
      `IndividualLeasingContractTest.activate_*`
- [ ] The full `terminate` guard table per party (I-08) —
      `IndividualLeasingContractTest.terminate_*`
- [ ] Attribution is recorded and never overwritten by a rejected or no-op
      termination — `IndividualLeasingContractTest.terminate_*_doesNotOverwriteAttribution`
- [ ] Attributed event per terminating party —
      `IndividualLeasingContractTest.terminate_byLessee_publishes*`,
      `.terminate_byMasterContract_publishes*`
- [ ] Event emission and drain semantics — `IndividualLeasingContractTest.pullDomainEvents_*`
- [ ] Persistence round-trip of every mutable property, and of every monetary
      property at scale 4 — `IndividualLeasingContractPersistenceAdapterIT`
- [ ] No setters, no framework import, no `Instant.now()` in the domain —
      `DependencyRulesTest`, `ClassRoleRulesTest` (ADR 0007)

### Open
- [ ] **`EXPIRED` is declared and reachable from no function.** Same shape as the
      master contract's `ENDED`: it needs a time-based trigger, which is `@Scheduled`
      and therefore an ADR (`sdd.playbook.md` § 6 item 6).
- [ ] **The return quota is not enforced.** `MlcConfiguration.returnQuota` and the
      early-claim window are inherited but no use case counts terminations against
      them, so an early-claim fee is never computed. Enforcing it requires counting
      terminations per contract per year, which is a cross-aggregate question and
      therefore a read-side projection plus a policy — not an invariant here.
- [ ] **`LIVE_LINKED` inheritance is unimplemented.** `documentation/notes.md`
      records why it is an ADR rather than an increment.
- [ ] **No optimistic locking.** Same gap as the master contract's.
